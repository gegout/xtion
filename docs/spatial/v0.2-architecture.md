# Spatial Vision 0.2 architecture

The source of truth is an immutable XTV-compatible depth frame and its capture clock. FrameSource supplies identical inputs for live capture, replay and synthetic tests. One perception worker owns the background model and tracks. A snapshot contains the frame, capture health, pixel quality, processing latency and a scene at that time. Freezing holds this snapshot while acquisition and perception continue publishing newer ones.

Camera coordinates are optical metres, X right, Y down, Z forward. The organised cloud borrows the source pixel grid. Axial Z can be metric with nominal optics; X and Y then remain approximate. Mount transforms are independent of the virtual camera. A user can accept an estimated floor height and pitch or enter a manual pose. Surface identity alone cannot distinguish a tabletop from a floor.

## Perception and geometry

The tracker uses measured changes from a learned background plus depth-consistent surface growth. When a previously measured patch meets a large unchanged surface, it can retain its directly measured samples. Sparse inferred regions require consistent depth ordering at their visible sides. Region summaries expose sample quality, median Z, visible XYZ bounds, and contact with the image boundary. Track association uses metric prediction, noise, direction consistency, image displacement and area. The velocity filter interpolates across short gaps internally. Exported trajectories contain actual observations only and keep the most recent 32 samples. Display latency and speed use host monotonic time.

Structural planes use inverse-depth fits on a grid. Neighbouring cells must agree in orientation and offset. The floor estimate uses a large near-horizontal plane below the sensor. This implements the planar-cell principle independently in Rust. CAPE also fits cylinders and performs a separate refinement stage. The metric tracker adapts observation-centric prediction repair to 3D centroids. OC-SORT describes a 2D detection tracker. Neither upstream implementation was embedded. [CAPE paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.02380) · [OC-SORT paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.14360)

## Reconstruction and display

The reconstruction module requires an organised metric cloud. Each rendered quad must have valid, continuous native-depth edges throughout its footprint and configurable border margin. Accepted quads become two triangles subject to a maximum metric edge length. Vertices retain their source pixels and area-weighted normals. Gaps and depth steps produce open boundaries. A separate worker accepts one pending reconstruction request; a newer frame replaces that request. Orbit and pan reproject the resulting mesh without changing sensor geometry.

Both point and surface views support CPU rendering and egui/glow drawing. The virtual camera supports perspective and orthographic projection. Surface picking resolves the visible triangle back to its source pixel with perspective-correct weights. Measurement checks the raw centre and uses nearby depths consistent with that surface. The uncertainty combines the configured axial noise model with recent depth variation at the pinned pixel. It does not quantify systematic optical calibration errors. Display colours encode depth or geometry.

## Extension points

Geometry export takes an immutable frame, matching scene, pose and reconstruction settings. Scene JSON carries shared metadata for the export directory. A future fusion or registration worker can consume sequences of organised clouds and camera poses without modifying FrameSource. A reconstruction consumer can likewise replace single-frame triangulation while keeping acquisition, replay, UI and AI independent. Optional AI sees a capped 8 KiB current spatial observation with short trajectories and measured track relationships. Network scheduling remains independent of rendering.

During warmup, the engine also records samples supported by structural planes in the reference scene. When unchanged floor or wall samples make up four fifths of a sparsely seeded region, the engine reconsiders its direct changed samples separately. It retains the two-observation stability check for this fallback. A moving patch can therefore meet a room plane and retain its own identity. The application loads optional noise-model overrides once at startup.
