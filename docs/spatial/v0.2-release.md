Tested on Ubuntu 26.04.1 LTS with the connected ASUS Xtion PRO, USB 1d27:0601, port 1-2, firmware 5.8.22. The driver crate remains 0.1.0. The current and baseline trackers saw identical recorded frames and scoring intervals. Personal depth recordings stay on the laptop.

## Isolated display repeat and tagged source checks

The renderer now projects each mesh vertex once per redraw and caches each face's depth sort key. An independent five-minute run of build a9160b3 used Xvfb with Wayland unset. It processed 8,875 frames at 29.63 FPS, with 31.28 active CPU draws/s and 31.21 draws/s over the full session. RSS at 10 seconds and the last sample was 149.8 and 152.5 MiB. Source counters were 83 gaps, 101 incomplete frames and 20 decode errors. The two runs differed in compositor visibility and concurrent work, so the change in rate cannot be attributed solely to projection reuse.

The tagged application sources (c2c5f24) include configurable noise and reference-plane exclusion. All seven clips reproduced the reported dominance, movement-event and ID-change counts. On the held-out outlines, entry coverage was 98.74% at 99.82% precision; occupied-start coverage was 99.98% at 96.52% precision. The occupied-start development outlines had 66.14% coverage. Each holdout contains three frames from the same recording.

Two additional live surface tests of the release sources drew 31.58/s on CPU and 28.85/s on glow. The five-restart test processed 134, 134, 134, 134, 134 frames across its five runs. The app sampling test exercised 5, 15 and 30 FPS on one open sensor. A separate final probe observed idle firmware and zero depth-endpoint bytes.

The comparison tool exited successfully on all seven clips. Benchmark timing reflects this laptop and software configuration. The numerical noise model measures repeatability; the near field lacks a device-specific fit. The depth camera sees only sensor-facing surfaces. Nominal optics, fragmented regions and tabletop/floor ambiguity limit interpretation of real measurements. The release includes local geometry and deterministic descriptions without requiring network access.
